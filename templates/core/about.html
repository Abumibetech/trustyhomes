How it works

A property marketplace for all of Nigeria.

TrustyHomes connects you directly with the agent, agency or property owner behind every listing — houses, land, offices, shops, warehouses and more, across all 36 states and the FCT. We provide the marketplace; you deal with each other directly.

For tenants & buyers

  1. Search by city, neighbourhood, category and budget.
  2. See real photos, videos, facilities and a map of the area.
  3. Message the agent or owner directly on WhatsApp, or call them.
  4. Request an inspection before you commit to anything.
  5. Save listings to favourites and share them with anyone.
  6. Report anything that looks suspicious — our admin team reviews every report.

For agents, owners & real estate companies

  • Free: up to 50 new uploads a day, no card required.
  • Basic — ₦5,000/month: up to 150 uploads a day.
  • Pro — ₦10,000/month: unlimited uploads + featured placement.
  • Business: custom plans for real estate companies managing many properties.

Everything above is free while we're launching — upgrade requests are handled directly with our team for now.

Trust & safety

We connect you directly with the person behind each listing — we don't inspect, manage or guarantee every property ourselves. Your biggest protection is the report button on every listing: enough reports, and our admin team steps in to review it and can hide it while they investigate.
